Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

For kids: WorthIt keeps everything on your computer. We never see your name, your wish list or the websites you visit. If you want something gone, ask a grown-up to remove it in WorthIt's settings.

WorthIt is an educational Chrome extension that teaches elementary school children what money is worth, by showing prices as days of their allowance. A parent or guardian sets it up. It is made by a student team in Singapore (Aadi, Dhruv, Arav, Arav and Madhav) as part of a Junior Achievement (JA) competition. In this policy, "we" means that team.

WorthIt is built so that no personal information leaves your computer. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics, so we do not collect, receive or store any personal data about you or your child. The one exception is optional: if a parent pays for the family plan, the payment companies receive that parent's email and payment details (see "Free week and family plan" below).

What WorthIt stores on your computer

Everything below is saved only in Chrome's local storage on the computer where WorthIt is installed. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

DataWhy
Allowance amount and how often it is paid (day, week or month), currency, and secondhand regionTo turn prices into days of allowance and show the right secondhand shops
Child's first name (optional)To say hi when WorthIt opens, for example "Hi Maya!"
Child's age (optional, chosen from 5 to "13 or older")To suggest a starting allowance and think-it-over wait during setup
Think-it-over wait (1, 3 or 7 days), on/off settings and whether the AI is onTo run the wish list and switch WorthIt or its AI on or off
Saving goal (optional): what the child is saving for, the goal amount and the amount saved so farTo compare a price with the goal and show how long saving would take
Which money lessons the child has finished, and their rankTo show progress on the Money Path and open the next lesson
Badges earned, the Starter Quest steps done, and the days the child used WorthIt to learn (only the dates, kept for the last 30 days)To show badges and a learning streak. There are no leaderboards and nothing is compared with other children
The date WorthIt was installed, and whether the family plan is activeTo run the free first week and unlock the family plan
Whether setup with Penny is finished, and whether the child made the "money promise" during setupSo setup isn't repeated, and to award the Money Promise badge
Wish list items the child chooses to save: product name, price and currency, the shop's web address for that product, and the date savedSo the child can wait, then decide, and a grown-up can open the product page

How long it is kept: until you delete it. Items can be removed from the wish list at any time, the settings page can clear the whole wish list, and removing WorthIt from Chrome (chrome://extensions → WorthIt → Remove) deletes everything. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete.

Shared computers: this data is not encrypted or password-protected. Anyone using the same Chrome profile can see the wish list and settings.

What WorthIt reads on web pages

When a shopping page opens, WorthIt looks for the product's price, name, page title and short description so it can show its panel. This happens entirely inside the browser. WorthIt does not record browsing history, does not store the pages you visit and does not read anything you type into websites. Only a product the child actively saves to the wish list is kept.

The AI in WorthIt

WorthIt includes a small AI model (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) inside the extension. When the child opens Better buy or Learn, it reads the product name, and the page's short description if there is one, to pick good search words and suitable shops. It runs on the computer itself; the text is not sent anywhere and is not stored. A parent can switch the AI off in WorthIt's settings.

On computers that support it, a parent can also turn on Chrome's own built-in AI (Gemini Nano). It also runs on the computer, inside Chrome, under Google's terms, and is only used to suggest a clearer product name and search words.

The AI never writes messages to the child. Every tip and lesson the child sees was written by the WorthIt team.

Penny, the guide

Penny is a friendly cartoon coin who walks the child and a grown-up through setup, gives a tour and answers common questions. During setup she asks for the child's first name, age, saving goal and allowance; every one of these is optional except the allowance, and the answers stay on the computer. She is not an AI chatbot. Every answer was written by the WorthIt team, children can only tap ready-made questions, there is no box to type in, and nothing tapped is recorded or sent anywhere.

The WorthIt web demo

The WorthIt demo website (aadiisthebest.github.io/worthit-app) works like the extension but saves nothing at all. Everything you type, including the sign-up email, name, age and pocket money, is held only in the open tab's memory and is gone the moment you refresh or close the tab. It is never written to your browser's storage, never sent anywhere and no account is created on any server. The password box is for show: it is not saved, checked or sent. Its checkout is a simulation: no card details are asked for and no payment is taken. The demo shop is pretend and sells nothing.

Links to other websites

Better buy and Save the Planet offer buttons that open real shops' own websites at a search for the product, for example Toys"R"Us, Popular, Decathlon, Challenger, Courts, Zalora, Amazon, Shopee, Lazada, Carousell or eBay. They open only when clicked, and send only the product name (or cleaned-up search words) to that website as a search term. Those websites have their own privacy and cookie policies. The panel reminds children to ask a grown-up before buying anything. WorthIt is not affiliated with, endorsed by or paid by any of these shops, and the links are not affiliate links.

Our website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs every visitor's IP address for security purposes; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We cannot see those logs. The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads its fonts from this site, not from a font service. Anything typed into the demo on the home page stays on that page and is gone when you close or reload it. See our Cookie Notice.

What we never do

  • We never send your data to ourselves or to anyone else.
  • No advertising, tracking, analytics or cookies.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data, ever.
  • No account or sign-up for children.
  • No purchases: WorthIt cannot buy anything.

Children's privacy

WorthIt is designed for children under 13 to use with a parent or guardian, who installs and sets it up. Because we do not collect personal data, no personal data about children reaches us. We built WorthIt with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) and the PDPC's Advisory Guidelines on Children's Personal Data in mind, as well as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If we ever need to collect a child's personal data, we will first get verifiable consent from a parent or guardian and update this policy.

Chrome Web Store

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. WorthIt uses only the storage permission, to save the settings and wish list listed above on your computer.

Free week and family plan

The first week is free and needs no card. To start it, a parent creates the family account on ExtensionPay's secure page by entering their email and the one-time code ExtensionPay sends; WorthIt then stays logged in on that computer and notes the day the free week started. (While payments are switched off, no account is needed at all.) On the free plan WorthIt also counts how many different products it has checked today (up to 3), on your computer, so it can apply the daily limit; the count resets each day and is never sent anywhere.

A parent (never a child) can subscribe from the welcome screen or from WorthIt's parent settings. The family account and payments are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. They receive the parent's email address and payment details so they can take payment, send receipts and let the parent log in on another computer. WorthIt then asks ExtensionPay whether the plan is active and keeps only that answer, the parent's email, whether the plan is monthly or yearly and any cancellation date, on your computer. We never see card numbers. Your child's name, settings, wish list, lessons and badges are never sent to ExtensionPay or Stripe and stay on your computer either way. Until payments are switched on, WorthIt does not contact ExtensionPay at all.

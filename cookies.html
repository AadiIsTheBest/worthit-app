Cookie Notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Short version: WorthIt uses no cookies. Not in the extension, and not on this website. That is why there is no cookie banner.

The extension

The WorthIt extension does not set or read cookies. Its settings and the child's wish list are saved in Chrome's own extension storage on your computer, only so WorthIt works. Nothing is sent to us. See the Privacy Policy for the full list.

This website

WhatUsed?
Cookies of any kindNo
Analytics, ads or tracking pixelsNo
Browser storage (local storage, session storage)No. The demo forgets everything when you reload the page.
Fonts or scripts from other companiesNo. Fonts and the demo's AI files load from this site.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Other websites

Shop and secondhand links open other companies' websites. Those websites may use cookies under their own cookie and privacy policies, which we do not control. When the family plan launches, its payment page will be run by ExtensionPay and Stripe, which may use cookies needed to take a payment securely; they are used only on that page, only by a parent who chooses to subscribe.

If this changes

If WorthIt ever needs cookies that are not strictly necessary, we will ask for permission first and update this notice.
